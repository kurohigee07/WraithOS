## When you need a commercial license

A commercial license is appropriate if:

- You want to embed Wraith (or a modified version) in a product whose
  source you cannot or will not disclose under AGPLv3.
- You run Wraith as a network service and cannot publish the
  corresponding source for modifications.
- You require explicit warranty, support, or indemnification that
  AGPLv3 does not provide.
- Your organisation's policy disallows AGPLv3 dependencies.

Merely using an unmodified Wraith binary internally, or distributing
unmodified binaries with attribution, is permitted under AGPLv3 and
does not require a commercial license.
